Projects

CLEAR

An educational mobile prototype exploring experimental skin-lesion image classification. It connects an Expo app to a FastAPI service and includes image upload validation and model evaluation.

Technologies: Python, PyTorch, FastAPI, TypeScript, React Native, and Expo.

Reading Motion in the Wild

A report exploring activity recognition from phone and smartwatch sensor data in UCSD's ExtraSensory dataset. It covers missing data, compares classification models, and presents interactive visualizations.

Methods and tools: logistic regression, ExtraTrees, scikit-learn, and Plotly.

GDP Nowcasting for the Turkish Economy

A Stata analysis that combines electricity consumption, bank credit, and card spending into a weekly economic index, then relates that index to quarterly Turkish GDP.

Methods and tools: Stata, principal component analysis, and ordinary least squares regression.
